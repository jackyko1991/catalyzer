# Data

Both files were downloaded unchanged from the RCSB Protein Data Bank. PDB archive data are free of all copyright
restrictions (CC0 1.0): <https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies>.

| File | Entry | What it is | Method, resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `1ema.pdb` | [1EMA](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1EMA) | Green fluorescent protein from *Aequorea victoria*, the bright S65T mutant; the chromophore is residue `CRO` 66 of chain A | X-ray, 1.9 Å |
| `2h5q.pdb` | [2H5Q](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2H5Q) | mCherry, a red fluorescent protein; its chromophore is residue `CH6` 66 | X-ray, 1.36 Å |

Both chromophores are made inside the protein itself, out of three of its own residues, which is why the file
records them as one modified residue (the `MODRES` lines name the residues they were made from).
